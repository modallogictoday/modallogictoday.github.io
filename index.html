Contemporary Modal Logic

Contemporary Modal Logic is a comprehensive collection focusing on recent developments in modal logic. Its chapters offer insights into different aspects of the field from mathematical, computational, informational and interactional, and philosophical perspectives. The chapters, typically 35 pages long, highlight recent advances and emerging trends.
